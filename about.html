ABOUT US

Our story

Who We Are

Otto Cafe opened its doors in 2021 in Ajman with one simple goal — to create a space where people could enjoy quality coffee without the fuss.

We believe that a great cup of coffee should be simple, honest and consistent. That is why we work with carefully selected beans and keep our menu focused on what we do best.

Our cafe has become a favourite spot for students, professionals and families across Ajman. We take pride in our friendly service and welcoming atmosphere.

Our Values

Quality

We never compromise on the quality of our ingredients and coffee.

Community

Otto is a place for everyone. We welcome all with a smile.

Simplicity

We keep things clean, simple and focused on what matters.
